noni

a chat app that runs small AI models on your device. no account, no tracking, and it works offline.

private

There's no account and no analytics. Your chats are saved on your device and nowhere else. noni only goes online to download models from Hugging Face.

several models in one chat

A small router model reads your message and passes it to the model that suits it best: general questions, code, or writing. In relay mode one model writes a draft and another polishes it. In council mode two models answer and a third combines their answers.

works offline

Once a model is downloaded, noni works in airplane mode. The models are free and open, and compressed to 4-bit so they stay small and reply quickly.

the starter pack is about 1.1 GB: qwen3 0.6b to route messages and llama 3.2 1b for everyday questions. you can add gemma 3, qwen2.5 coder and larger llama models from the models tab.
