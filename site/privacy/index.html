privacy policy

last updated november 2, 2026 · this is the same policy shipped inside the app (settings ▸ about ▸ privacy policy).

the short version

noni does not collect, store, or send any personal data. Your conversations stay on your device, and noni never sends anything you type anywhere. Like other app data, your conversations are included in your own device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have them turned on.

on-device processing

noni runs small open-weight language models on your device's own processor. Your messages, the AI's replies, conversation titles and all settings are processed and stored only on your device. There is no noni server: no account, no sign-in, no sync and no cloud processing of any kind.

what noni does not do

  • no analytics or telemetry of any kind
  • no advertising and no ad identifiers
  • no tracking across apps or websites
  • no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • no reading of your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or any other personal information

model downloads

noni uses the network only to get models: when you download one, and when you add one from a Hugging Face link (noni reads that model's public listing to check it can run it). Model files are downloaded over HTTPS directly from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), the public site that hosts them. These downloads are anonymous: noni sends no account information or identifiers. As with any download, Hugging Face's servers can see your IP address while sending the files. Their privacy policy is at huggingface.co/privacy. Loading and chatting with a downloaded model never uses the network, so it works fully offline.

notifications

If you allow notifications (noni asks when you first open it), noni can tell you when a reply paused because you left the app. The notification is scheduled on your device. There is no notification server, and the notification never contains your messages.

your data and how to delete it

Your conversations are stored only in noni's storage on your device. You can delete a conversation with a swipe, erase everything with "clear all chats" in settings, or remove all app data by deleting the app. Downloaded models can be removed on the models tab. Like other app data, conversations are included in your device's backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer. Those backups are kept by Apple or on your computer, never by noni, and downloaded models are not backed up. We hold no copy of anything, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

ai-generated content

Replies are generated on your device by open-weight language models. Like all language models, they can be wrong or unexpected. Nobody reviews generated content, and it never leaves your device. If a reply is harmful or inappropriate, you can report it from the message's long-press menu. The report opens as an email in your own mail app for you to review and send; noni itself sends nothing.

children

noni is not directed at children and is rated for adults (18+) because AI-generated text can be unpredictable.

changes to this policy

If noni's privacy practices ever change, this policy will be updated in the app and at noni.farm/privacy before the change takes effect.
