support

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to support@noni.farm. noni is made by one developer, so replies are personal but can take a day or two.

why is the first download about 1.1 GB?

That's the size of the two models in the starter pack: qwen3 0.6b (about 350 MB), which routes your messages, and llama 3.2 1b (about 710 MB), which answers everyday questions. They download directly from Hugging Face. Wi-Fi is best. Downloads keep going in the background and pick up where they left off if they're interrupted.

does noni work offline?

Yes. Once a model is downloaded, everything runs on your device, even in airplane mode. noni only uses the network to download models.

why is the first reply slow?

The first message after you open the app loads the model into memory, which can take a few seconds. After that, replies start almost right away, at about 10 to 30 tokens per second on recent devices. Small local models are slower and simpler than big cloud models, but nothing you type leaves your device.

how do i delete models or free up space?

Open the models tab. Each downloaded model shows its size and a delete button, and the total is shown at the top. Deleting a model frees the space right away, and you can download it again any time. Larger models such as qwen3 1.7b and llama 3.2 3b need more memory and are marked in the list.

how do i report a harmful or inappropriate reply?

Long-press the reply and tap report. An email to support@noni.farm opens with the reply and a few app details filled in. You can edit it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. You can also use "report a problem" in settings ▸ about.

how do i delete all my data?

Swipe to delete a conversation, use "clear all chats" in settings, or delete the app. All of noni's data is on your device and there's no copy on a server, so deleting the app removes everything.
